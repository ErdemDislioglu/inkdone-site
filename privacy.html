Inkdone Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Türkçe

Inkdone is an app for planning your tasks like a notebook. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: Inkdone collects no personal data, sends nothing anywhere and shares nothing with anyone.

What is stored?

Your tasks, repeating tasks, reminders and settings are stored only on your own device, in the app's own storage. The home screen widget reads this data on the device to show today's tasks.

What is collected or shared?

Nothing. Inkdone has no sign-up, account, cloud backup, ads, analytics or crash reporting. The app does not connect to the internet. No one, including the developer, can access your data.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device by its own notification system. Allowing notifications is optional; the app keeps working without them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app to permanently delete all your data at any time. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Because the data exists only on your device, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Children

Inkdone collects no data from users of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
